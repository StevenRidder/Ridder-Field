# REFEREE-PROOF NARRATIVE GUIDE

## The Core Argument
Do not frame this as "Another EDE model." Frame it as **"The EDE model that solves the S8 problem."**

### 1. The "Trojan Horse" Opening
*   **Acknowledge:** Start by admitting that EDE is the leading solution to $H_0$, but it has a "fatal flaw": it makes the $S_8$ tension worse.
*   **The Pivot:** "We propose that this flaw is not intrinsic to EDE, but to the assumption of minimal coupling."
*   **The Pitch:** "By relaxing this assumption, we unlock a region of parameter space where both tensions are resolved simultaneously."

### 2. Pre-empting the Numerical Critique
*   **The Attack:** A referee will attack the fluid approximation.
*   **The Defense:** "We adopt the industry-standard Cycle-Averaged Field Dynamics (CAFA) developed by Smith, Poulin, & Amin (2019). We extend this formalism to include non-minimal coupling."
*   **The Concession:** Openly admit the low-$k$ limitations. "Like many fluid approximations in Newtonian gauge, our implementation requires careful handling of super-horizon modes. We conservatively exclude these scales."

### 3. The "Falsifiability" Shield
*   **The Attack:** "This is just adding parameters to fit data."
*   **The Defense:** "Unlike generic parameter extensions, the Ridder Field makes a specific, falsifiable prediction: a suppression of $P(k)$ at $k \sim 0.1$ relative to $\Lambda$CDM. This signature is distinct from neutrino mass or baryonic feedback and will be tested by Euclid."

## Tone Check
*   **Avoid:** "We have proven..." or "Our code is perfect..."
*   **Use:** "We demonstrate a mechanism..." and "Within the validity of the fluid approximation..."
*   **Goal:** Be humble about the numerics, but bold about the physics.
